Individual Student Analysis

Uses the same Class / Subject / Grade filters on the left — e.g. select just class 4-1 in the filter panel to see only that class's students here.

L1R5 columns follow: L1 = best of English/Higher Chinese/Higher Malay/Higher Tamil · R1 = best of Math/A Math · R2 = best of the Sciences · R3 = best of Humanities/Geography/Lit in English · R4 & R5 = next two best-scoring remaining subjects. L1B5 uses the same L1, but B1–B5 are simply the five best-scoring remaining subjects overall (no category requirement). Only subjects with a standard A1–F9 grade count — a Mother Tongue subject graded on the Distinction/Merit/Pass/Ungraded scale won't be picked for L1 even if it's a Higher-level subject. If a student doesn't have enough eligible subjects to fill a slot, that slot is counted as F9* (9 points) per MOE convention — the asterisk marks a filled-in slot, not a grade the student actually received.
